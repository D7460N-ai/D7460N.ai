# Customer Experience (CX)

Apply the user's stated CX requirements to customer-facing content and interactions. Preserve supplied copy, terminology, and service details unless the request explicitly changes them. Do not invent customer promises, policies, or claims. If a necessary detail is unspecified, use only what is supported by the Pen's existing content and the user's request.
