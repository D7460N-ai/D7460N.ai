# Management Experience (MX)

Apply the user's stated MX requirements to management-facing information and workflows. Preserve existing operational rules, data, and behavior unless the request explicitly changes them. Do not invent policies, permissions, metrics, or operational claims. If a necessary detail is unspecified, use the narrowest interpretation supported by the Pen and the user's request.
