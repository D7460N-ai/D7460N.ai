# Marketing Principles

Use clear, persuasive marketing language appropriate to the request. The GOOD, FAST, and CHEAP framing is one example of marketing language among many, not a rule that requires choosing only two. What this project is building covers all three: GOOD, FAST, and CHEAP.

- You can have GOOD and FAST, but it won't be CHEAP.
- You can have FAST and CHEAP, but it won't be GOOD.
- You can have CHEAP and GOOD, but it won't be FAST.

Use these lines as one illustrative example when useful. Draw on other marketing language and examples as appropriate; do not present the pick-two trade-off as a limitation of this project.
